07 / THE LOGIC

How the model works

Four steps take the strategy from a stock universe to a research portfolio.

01 / UNIVERSE

Start with tradable stocks

Screen NSE index constituents for liquidity using about three months of average daily trading value.

02 / SELECTION

Find sustained momentum

Rank six- and twelve-month price strength, skipping the latest 21 trading days. Select 5–15 eligible stocks.

03 / WEIGHTS

Size for volatility

Give less weight to more volatile stocks. An optional stock 200-day screen can remove names below their trend.

04 / REVIEW

Refresh the model basket

Use the latest completed close to show the selected stocks, target weights and any cash allocation.

WHEN THE NIFTY TREND WEAKENS

Choose how much to hold

The market signal compares the Nifty 50 with its 200-day average. Your risk choice changes what happens below it.

Low Move to cashSell model holdings and pause new purchases until the market filter clears.

Moderate Keep five already ownedRank this model’s existing positions by momentum, retain the strongest five and hold the rest in cash. It does not buy replacements during risk-off.

Aggressive Keep selectingIgnore the market filter and continue buying the top eligible stocks at monthly reviews.

Moderate and aggressive are separate paths. After a risk-off period, aggressive may own new stocks that moderate never bought; moderate’s five are not necessarily five of today’s aggressive basket.

READ THE RESULTS CAREFULLY

Backtests are research, not a return promise.

The saved configuration was selected using historical data. Current index membership, execution assumptions and changing market conditions can make simulated results too optimistic. The three-year windows reuse that selected configuration and are not independent prospective results. Market prices and constituents are provided by external data sources.
